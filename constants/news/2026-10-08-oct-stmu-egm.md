# October STMU and EGM

**When**: 28th October - times to be confirmed

**Where**: Informatics Forum G.07

We'll be holding our next STMU and EGM soon! The EGM is where we elect unfilled roles for the rest of the year.

## STMU

Details for the STMU will be updated soon - stay tuned!

## EGM

### Roles up for election

- 1st Year Rep
  - Responsible for communication between their year and the Society, and promotes events to their year.
  - Responsible for organising some of CompSoc's large projects and events, such as InfBall.
  - Gain an insight into the day to day of running CompSoc, and valuable experience in organising large scale society events.

- Masters Year Rep - As above

- Junior Treasurer
  - Manage daily financial operations - including making reimbursements, maintaining budgets, and tracking transactions.
  - Gain hands-on experience in the financials of managing a £100,000+ balance sheet.
  - Obtain valuable insights into financial management and prepare for a potential future role as Treasurer.

### How do I run?

To run for one of these roles:

- [Submit a manifesto here](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/ZGTqUyw4wi) - you can also run on the day but it's a good idea to write a manifesto. All manifestos should be submitted before the 28th.

- Prepare a speech:
  - 1st Year Rep - **2 minutes**
  - All other roles - **strictly 1 minute**

### Voting

Voting will be in person and online - **you'll need to have membership on EUSA**, you can [sign up for free here](https://www.eusa.ed.ac.uk/activities/view/compsoc)

See you there!
