c:T8d8,<h1>October STMU and EGM</h1>
<p><strong>When</strong>: 28th October - times to be confirmed</p>
<p><strong>Where</strong>: Informatics Forum G.07</p>
<p>We'll be holding our next STMU and EGM soon! The EGM is where we elect unfilled roles for the rest of the year.</p>
<h2>STMU</h2>
<p>Details for the STMU will be updated soon - stay tuned!</p>
<h2>EGM</h2>
<h3>Roles up for election</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>1st Year Rep</p>
<ul>
<li>Responsible for communication between their year and the Society, and promotes events to their year.</li>
<li>Responsible for organising some of CompSoc's large projects and events, such as InfBall.</li>
<li>Gain an insight into the day to day of running CompSoc, and valuable experience in organising large scale society events.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Masters Year Rep - As above</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Junior Treasurer</p>
<ul>
<li>Manage daily financial operations - including making reimbursements, maintaining budgets, and tracking transactions.</li>
<li>Gain hands-on experience in the financials of managing a £100,000+ balance sheet.</li>
<li>Obtain valuable insights into financial management and prepare for a potential future role as Treasurer.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Social Media Officer</p>
<ul>
<li>Responsible for creating engaging content for CompSoc's 2,000+ combined social media followers.</li>
<li>Work closely with CompSoc's Executive Committee and Graphic Designer to grow CompSoc's online presence and create effective campaigns to advertise events and job opportunities.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>How do I run?</h3>
<p>To run for one of these roles:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><a href="https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/ZGTqUyw4wi">Submit a manifesto here</a> - you can also run on the day but it's a good idea to write a manifesto. All manifestos should be submitted before the 28th.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Prepare a speech:</p>
<ul>
<li>1st Year Rep - <strong>2 minutes</strong></li>
<li>All other roles - <strong>strictly 1 minute</strong></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Voting</h3>
<p>Voting will be in person and online - <strong>you'll need to have membership on EUSA</strong>, you can <a href="https://www.eusa.ed.ac.uk/activities/view/compsoc">sign up for free here</a></p>
<p>See you there!</p>
